Precision-Recall Curve

Preferred over the ROC curve as a summary under class imbalance: it directly shows how precision degrades as recall increases, rather than the false-positive *rate*, which can stay visually small even while the absolute false-positive count (and therefore precision) collapses.

TPR at a Fixed False-Positive-Rate Budget

The primary-model selection criterion used in this project (see docs/research-notes.md, Experiment D): for a human-in-the-loop triage tool, how much real detection signal survives at a false-alarm rate an analyst could actually tolerate matters more than a full-curve average that includes unrealistic operating points.

Dataset & Training Details

Train/validation/test splits are grouped by source photograph (see ml/training/splitting.py) so that no crop — or any stego derivative of it — appears in more than one split. This prevents the model from memorizing a specific photo's noise fingerprint instead of learning general steganalysis signals.
